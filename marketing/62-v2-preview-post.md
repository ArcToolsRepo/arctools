# 62 — v2 preview (whole site rebuilt beside the live one)

## Main post

There is a second ArcTools now, and you can open it without leaving the first.

arctools.fun/trade2

Every page was rebuilt into one terminal frame: a rail down the left with every launchpad we index, the chart and the trades in the middle, the stats and the trade panel on the right. Fifteen pages moved across — Terminal, Swap, token pages, Portfolio, Wallets, Rewards, Launchpad, Pay, Bridge, Insiders, Traders, Scanner, Intel, Profile, Referrals.

Clicking a launchpad in the rail does what it says: it opens the Terminal filtered to that source. New pairs, gainers and the watchlist are one click from the same rail.

The live site did not change. It is still the default, still at the same addresses, and every link you already have keeps pointing at it. v2 is opt-in: there is a visible v1/v2 switch on both sides that carries the page, the filters and the anchor across, so you land on the same view in the other interface. Nothing redirects on its own.

It is a preview, so it is where the rough edges live. Tell us what breaks.
