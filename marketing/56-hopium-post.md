# 56 — Hopium added to the Terminal

## Main post

New launchpad in the Terminal: Hopium (@hopium_gg).

Hopium launches a token, seeds a Uniswap v4 pool and locks the position in one transaction — no bonding curve, no graduation step. Pairs can be USDC or a tokenized stock, and the creator picks a 3–10% trading fee with 2% going to the platform.

Everything Hopium has launched is now in ArcTools: its own source chip, every coin with logo, live price, score and a one-click buy that routes straight through the Hopium pool. 15 launches indexed at the time of writing, 27 launchpads in one table.

Metadata comes from the token contracts themselves — Hopium writes logo, description and links on-chain, so what you see is what the creator signed.
