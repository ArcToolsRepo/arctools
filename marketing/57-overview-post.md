# 57 — What ArcTools is (overview)

## Main post

Everything on Arc, in one place.

**Terminal** — arctools.fun/trade
27 launchpads in one table: ArcPad, Warp, Tolly, Arguspad, UBI.fun, Minara, Klik, faze.fun, peach.ag, creo.family, pools.trade, Hopium, Lift, eve.fun, Ellipse, Sashimi, aka.fun, DYORSwap, Uniswap V3/V4 and the stock wrappers. Risk score, dev and bundle exposure, smart money, live trades. One click buys at the best route across venues.

**Sniper bot** — @ArcSniper_bot
Snipe a launch in the block it opens. Limit, TP, SL, copy-trade, wallet alerts, positions and PnL in the chat. Sell tax ignored by design: on Arc speed decides.

**Pay links** — arctools.fun/pay
Send USDC with a link. The person collecting needs no wallet and no gas; uncollected links return to you. Every link is its own on-chain record.

**Trader profiles** — arctools.fun/leaderboard
Your on-chain record with a name on it: PnL, ROI, win rate computed from indexed swaps, wallets attached by signature, your buys marked on the charts. Follow and copy the ones that actually win.

**Launchpad** — arctools.fun/launchpad
Launch a coin in one transaction, instant pool or bonding curve, liquidity locked, part of every fee to ARCT stakers.

**Bridge and rewards** — arctools.fun/bridge
USDC in from any CCTP chain, stake ARCT and earn the platform's fees. The burn counter runs on real buybacks, not promises.

All of it reads one index: every swap on Arc, decoded from our own node.
