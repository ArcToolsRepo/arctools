## Long version (X Premium)

When an Arc KOL tweets about a token, the chart shows who.

Every token page on arctools.fun now carries three layers of X data on top of the on-chain ones:

KOL avatars on the chart
The moment a tracked KOL posts about a token, their profile picture is pinned to that exact candle. Hover for followers and the tweet text, click to open the post. Mentions are matched by contract address, by unique $cashtag with Arc context, or by the project's own X handle. Celebrity and ecosystem handles pasted into token metadata do not count.

Smart followers
Which Arc KOLs follow the token's X account, with follower counts, plus the account's age. Zero KOLs and a weeks-old account is the usual shape of a throwaway launch.

KOL mentions
The tweets themselves under the chart: author, followers, likes, views, time.

How the KOL database is built
Accounts with 10k+ followers that post about Arc are discovered from X automatically every day. Ecosystem accounts (Arc, Circle, launchpads, explorers) are seeded by hand. Their follow graphs and mentions are refreshed continuously, so the list grows with the ecosystem. Today it tracks 130+ accounts; new KOLs are added as they start posting about Arc.

Same page still has: dev / insider / pro-wallet badges, Token Score, deployer history, My position, Top traders, one-click buy through the aggregator.
