# 49 — Trader profiles

## Main post

Trader profiles are live on ArcTools.

Your record on Arc, computed from the chain, with a name and a picture on top of it. You cannot type a number into it — every figure comes from swaps we indexed.

How it works:
- a wallet joins a profile by signing a message; no signature, no claim
- up to 8 wallets per profile, PnL summed
- leaderboard by PnL, ROI on volume, win rate and volume, with weekly, monthly and all-time seasons
- your buys show up on token charts as your avatar; a badges toggle in the chart toolbar hides them when you want a clean view
- the public trade feed is delayed by default, so your own followers cannot front-run you

We seeded the board with the five top insider wallets by 30-day PnL. Those pages are marked unclaimed: the numbers are real, the name is a placeholder, and one signature from the wallet makes the page yours.

Search a trader by name in the Terminal search box, or start here:
arctools.fun/leaderboard
