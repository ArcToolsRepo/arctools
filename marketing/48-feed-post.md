# 48 — Arc feed (X voices + crypto news, buy button per post)

## Main post

New in the Terminal: Arc feed.

The right column now carries what Arc people are posting on X and what crypto media is publishing — next to the table you trade in, with a live lamp when the collectors are running.

When a post names an Arc token, that post gets its own buy button. One click, same aggregator, same local signing as any row.

The button only appears when the ticker resolves to exactly one contract. A bare cashtag counts only with Arc context in the post, because $CAT exists on every chain and a buy button pointing at the wrong token is worse than no button.

Accounts are picked by how much they actually post about Arc, not by follower count. Outlets stay in the News tab where they belong.

140 posts indexed, 227 headlines, 4 news sources.
