# 82 — ARCT USDC reflections (RadarDex)

## Main post

Hold ARCT. Get paid in USDC.

The ARCT contract has a 1 % buy / 1 % sell tax. Half of it goes to every holder as USDC through RadarDex reflections, split by your share of supply. No staking, no lock, nothing to sign up for.

Claim it any time on the RadarDex token page - the button says "Claim X USDC" - or it simply arrives on your next transfer or sell.

Paid out so far: 3,839 USDC to holders, 3,839 USDC to the ArcTools treasury (operators, buyback and burn), 37.05M ARCT burned by the tax itself.

The other half is not hidden: it is the treasury share, and the burn counter is public at arctools.fun/rewards.
