# 70 — ArcOne 2.2

## Main post

ArcOne 2.2 is out — and from now on you update from inside the app.

Settings → App updates → Update. The app downloads the signed build, verifies it and opens the installer. No browser, no re-downloading from the site. Your wallet and settings stay.

Also in 2.2:
- Launchpad chips: tap a launchpad and see every token from it, newest first
- Real token age: mint time from the explorer, never younger than the first trade
- Correct market caps: burned supply excluded — no more $1.5M caps on $50K tokens
- Logos everywhere: ArcToolsPad and site-hosted logos now show in the app
- Honest screens: no endless loading; clear messages, and a screen that breaks tells you why

On 1.8–2.1: Settings → App updates → Update. On older builds, or if you are new: arctools.fun/app — this is the last time you need the site for it.
