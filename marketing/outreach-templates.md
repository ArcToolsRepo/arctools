# Arc outreach — ready-to-send messages

Never open with "buy our token". On a chain this small, one spam message burns the
relationship permanently. Every message below leads with something free.

---

## 1. DM to a project owner / group admin (the main one)

> Hey — I run ArcTools (arctools.fun), the trading stack for Arc.
>
> I'd like to give your group our buy-alert bot for free. It posts every buy of $TICKER
> in your chat with your logo, your socials, the buyer's size and the token's rank —
> the same thing groups on other chains pay $50-200/month for.
>
> It also puts $TICKER on our public trending board and tracks your holders in our
> smart-money leaderboard, so your community can see which wallets are accumulating.
>
> Takes one minute: add @ArcToolsBuyBot to the group as admin, send /add <your CA>.
> Nothing to pay, no strings. If it's noisy you set a minimum buy size or remove it.

Why it works: you are handing them infrastructure they cannot build. And every alert
carries ArcTools branding to their members forever.

---

## 2. Follow-up 48h later (only if they installed it)

> Alerts have been running for two days — anything you want changed? I can add a
> minimum buy size, custom emoji, media on big buys, or a milestone message.
>
> One more thing you might want: if you ever launch another token, ArcToolsPad gives
> you the launch for free (we even pay the gas), plus these alerts and a trending slot
> from block one.

does. In degen groups the data earns the right to talk about the token later.

## Order of operations

1. MALA first (biggest audience, already our user, warmest lead)
2. @Arc_Alpha_Degens data post (only chain-wide degen venue, 372 online)
3. The 15 groups with 100+ members from arc-outreach.md, top down
4. Official Discord + the launchpads (slower, but this is where partnerships live)
5. Everything else in the list, 10 DMs a day

Track it: token, handle, date sent, installed y/n. Twenty installs is the whole game —
that is roughly 4,000 people seeing ArcTools branding every time somebody buys.
