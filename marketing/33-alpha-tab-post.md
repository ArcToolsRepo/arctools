## Long version (X Premium, single post)

The ArcTools Terminal has a new tab: Alpha.

Instead of sorting by volume, it scores every token on Arc from our own swap index (244k swaps, 12.9k wallets) and shows three lists:

Fresh alpha. Tokens under one hour old where top-100 wallets are already entering. Usually empty. It lights up when it matters: this afternoon ACTOR hit score 90 at 30 minutes old with 16 top-100 wallets and a cluster of 9 insiders inside 15 minutes.

Accumulation. Older tokens where insiders keep buying for hours while the price has not run yet. This is the mode with the strongest backtest.

Revival. A token that went quiet for hours and is waking up on real volume from several wallets. Experimental, small sample.

Every pick carries its reasons as chips: how many top-100 wallets bought and how much, cluster size, unique-buyer acceleration, buy flow share, KOL mentions, dev and bundle status. Hard filters remove dev over 25%, bundle over 20%, any dev selling and deployers with a rug history.

Backtest before launch, 5 days, 149,639 swaps, 193 cutoffs, the scorer only sees data before each cutoff:
- Accumulation 75+: 2% rug rate within 24 h vs 39% for a random Arc token. 42% up more than 10% after 24 h vs 11% baseline.
- Fresh 75+: 13% doubled vs 2% baseline. Median still negative: better odds, still a lottery.

Top 3 picks in every mode are free. The full list unlocks for wallets staking 10,000 ARCT, same gate as the Insiders board. Refreshes every 45 seconds.
