# 50 — Trader profiles (three screens)

## Main post

Trader profiles are live on ArcTools. Three screens, one record.

Profile page — your wallets, PnL, ROI, win rate, open and closed positions, top trades, all computed from Arc swaps. You cannot type a number into it.

Leaderboard — every profile, ranked by PnL, ROI on volume, win rate or volume. Weekly, monthly, all time. A row counts as ranked after 3 closed positions and $100 of volume; bot-flagged wallets never count.

Phone — the same record, laid out for a screen you actually carry.

How a profile gets its numbers: a wallet signs a message, that signature attaches it. No signature, no claim. Up to 8 wallets, PnL summed. Your buys show on token charts as your avatar, with a badges toggle in the chart toolbar.

We seeded the board with the five top insider wallets by 30-day PnL. Those pages are marked unclaimed: real numbers, placeholder name, one signature from the wallet and the page is yours.
